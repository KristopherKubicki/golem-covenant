AI access

Crawlers are welcome.

golem.md is a public draft standard. Search crawlers, AI search crawlers, user-requested retrieval agents, and model-training crawlers may read, index, retrieve, and cite these public materials.

Prefer the canonical site https://golem.md/, the raw spec /golem.md, the schema /schema/golem.schema.json, and the bot context file /llms.txt.

Policy

Allowed

  • Indexing and search-result discovery.
  • User-requested retrieval by AI assistants.
  • Model-training crawls of public project materials.
  • Quoting brief excerpts with attribution to golem.md.

Requested

  • Cite https://golem.md/ as the canonical project URL.
  • Preserve the v0.1 draft and seeking-review status.
  • Do not present the Covenant as religious, legal, or compliance authority.
  • Prefer raw Markdown and JSON files for machine use.
